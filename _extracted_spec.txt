1. Overview
Xacqora Track is our own lead tracker and attribution CRM. It shows, for every lead, which ad brought them in, every ad they clicked after that, where they stand in the funnel right now, and whether they paid. It sends the real outcomes back to Meta so Meta finds more buyers, not just more form fillers.
The problem today
Leads sit in Google Sheets, Pabbly logs, TagMango, Aevent and WhatsApp. Nobody sees one lead's full story in one place.
We match sales to ads by hand every week. It is slow and breaks when phone numbers or UTMs are messy.
Meta only learns from the Lead event. It never hears who attended, who booked a call, or who paid.
Clients ask "how are my leads doing" and we answer with screenshots and sheets.
What Xacqora Track does
Captures every click, form fill and Meta Instant Form lead with its exact campaign, ad set and ad.
Joins every event about that person into one lead record, keyed on phone number.
Shows the lead's live status: registered, attended, booked a call, paid, refunded.
Pulls spend from all our Meta ad accounts and calculates cost and profit per campaign, ad set and ad.
Sends qualified events back to Meta through the Conversions API.
Gives each client a login to see their own leads and numbers.
Who it is for
Phase 1 to 3 is internal: Foremost Leads runs it on our own client accounts. Phase 4 adds the client portal. Selling it as SaaS to other coaches comes after we prove the data on 3 to 4 clients. Every design choice should still assume many clients (multi-tenant) from day one, so we do not rebuild later.
Who builds it
Our own team, using AI coding tools. Section 8 covers how.

Identity rules
Normalise every phone to +91XXXXXXXXXX format before saving. Strip spaces, 0 and 91 prefixes.
Match on phone first, then email (lowercase, trimmed).
When a known visitor cookie fills a form, attach all earlier anonymous touchpoints of that visitor to the lead.
Same phone, different client = different leads. Never merge across clients.
Keep raw_payload of every incoming webhook for debugging and re-processing.
5. Module requirements
Ten modules. Each one is shippable on its own and listed in build order.
5.1 Tracking script (xt.js)
One line of JS pasted in the head of any landing page: Lovable, FlexiFunnels, TagMango pages, WordPress, custom.
On load: read all UTMs, fbclid, campaign_id, adset_id, ad_id from the URL. Create or read a first-party visitor_id cookie (365 days). Create fbc from fbclid if missing. Read fbp.
Send a touchpoint to our API on every landing.
Auto-fill hidden fields in any form on the page (utm_*, ad_id, visitor_id, fbc, fbp) so they travel with the form submit.
Append the same values to outgoing links and checkout buttons (TagMango, Cashfree, Razorpay pages) so attribution survives the redirect.
Under 10 KB, no impact on page speed, works without our server being up (queue and retry).
5.2 Meta Instant Form leads
Connect a Facebook Page through Meta login. Subscribe to the leadgen webhook.
On each lead: fetch the lead fields, ad_id, adset_id, campaign_id, form_id. Create or match the Lead.
Field mapping screen per form (which question is phone, name, city, custom questions).
5.3 Identity and lead record
Apply the identity rules in Section 4.
Duplicate review screen: show likely duplicates (same email, different phone) for a human to merge.
5.4 Lead timeline (the main screen)
Header: name, phone, email, client, current stage, owner, total paid.
Source card: first ad clicked, last ad clicked, count of ad clicks, with the ad thumbnail and name.
Timeline: every event, newest first, with platform icon and time in IST.
Search by phone, email, name. Filters by client, funnel, stage, campaign, ad, date range.
5.5 Pipeline and sales
Stages per funnel, editable. Default for a webinar funnel: New, Registered, Attended, Hot, Call Booked, Paid, Lost.
Stages move automatically from events (webinar_joined moves to Attended) and can be moved by hand.
Lead assignment to reps: round robin or by rule. Tasks, notes, call outcomes, next follow-up date.
Kanban view and list view. Bulk actions: assign, change stage, export CSV.
5.6 Integrations hub
One inbound webhook URL per client per platform, with a secret.
Mapping screen: which field in the payload is phone, email, amount, product.
Every webhook logged with status (processed, failed, duplicate) and a retry button.
Full list and event mapping in Section 6.
5.7 Meta Ads pull
Connect multiple ad accounts per client through Meta Business login (system user token preferred).
Every night and every 3 hours during the day: pull campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, and daily insights per ad (spend, impressions, reach, clicks, link clicks, ThruPlays).
Store spend in billing currency and in INR, using a stored daily FX rate.
5.8 Meta CAPI push
Per client: choose which pixel/dataset gets which events.
Send Lead, CompleteRegistration (registered), custom Attended, Schedule (call booked), Purchase with value.
Include hashed phone (SHA-256, E.164 without +), hashed email, fbc, fbp, client IP, user agent, event_id for dedup with the browser pixel.
Retry queue. Show Event Match Quality and send status per event.
Rule: never send an event twice. Never send Purchase for a refunded payment; send a refund note in our records instead.
5.9 Reports
Covered in Section 7.
5.10 Client portal and admin
Client login sees only their own leads, funnels and reports. Read-only by default.
Our roles: Owner, Admin, Media Buyer, Sales Rep, Viewer.
Phone numbers masked for roles that do not need them (show last 4 digits).
Audit log of who viewed, exported or changed what.

Rules for every integration
Pass UTMs and visitor_id through to the checkout or form wherever the platform allows custom fields, so the sale carries its own attribution.
Use the gateway transaction ID to stop the same payment being counted twice.
A payment with no matching lead creates a new lead marked "source unknown". It still counts in revenue.
Every platform has a test button that sends a fake event and shows where it landed.
7. Attribution rules and reports
Every sale gets credited to one ad by a fixed rule, and every report states which rule it used. Default is last paid click within 30 days, because webinar buyers often pay 1 to 3 weeks after the first click.
Attribution models (selectable per report)

Use for

Judging which ads find new people

Long journeys with many retargeting clicks
Lead source
The ad on the lead's first form fill
Matching what our current weekly sheet does
Window is editable per client (7, 14, 30, 60 days). Sales with no ad click in the window go to "Organic / unknown".
Reports in the first version
Funnel report per client: ad spend, leads, registrations, attended, calls booked, sales, revenue. Cost per lead, per registrant, per attendee, per call, per sale. Show-up rate and close rate.
Ad performance table: campaign, ad set, ad rows with spend, leads, sales, revenue, ROAS, profit. Drill down from campaign to ad. Ad thumbnail on every row.
Wasted spend: ads above a set spend with zero sales, sorted by spend.
New vs old leads: sales from leads created inside the date range vs older leads.
Meta vs Xacqora Track: Meta-reported conversions next to our counted conversions per ad, so we see where Meta over or under reports.
Lead source mix: Instant Form vs landing page vs organic vs unknown.
Weekly client summary: one page, auto-generated every Monday 9 AM IST, sent to the client portal and as a PDF.
Every report can export CSV and Excel. Every number links to the list of leads behind it. Revenue counts net of refunds.

10. Security, privacy and compliance
We will hold phone numbers and payment records of lakhs of people for many clients, so one leak hurts every client at once. These rules are not optional in any phase.
Security checklist
☐ Row-level security: every query is filtered by client_id at the database level, not only in the app code
☐ Meta tokens, gateway secrets and webhook secrets stored encrypted, never in code or in the frontend
☐ Every inbound webhook verified by signature or secret before processing
☐ 2-factor login for all Foremost Leads users
☐ Phone and email masked for roles that do not need them; exports logged in the audit log
☐ Daily database backups, restore tested once a month
☐ Rate limits on the public ingest API so a bad actor cannot flood it
Privacy and law
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies. The legal or compliance owner confirms consent wording and data retention rules before the pilot goes live.
Each client's landing page and lead form carries a privacy notice and consent line that covers tracking and sharing with Meta.
Phone and email are hashed with SHA-256 before sending to Meta. Raw values never leave our servers.
A client can ask us to delete a lead; deletion removes it from our database and stops any future CAPI events for that person.
Meta platform terms: we only use Meta data for the ad account owner's own reporting. Meta Lead Form data is never shared between clients.
